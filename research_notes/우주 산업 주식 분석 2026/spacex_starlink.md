## KQ1. IPO status, timeline, structure, valuation (private tenders 2024–2026 → listing)

### Takeaway
SpaceX is no longer "soon-investable" — it is listed. The whole company (Falcon/Starship launch + Starlink + the former xAI, now "SpaceXAI") priced its IPO at $135/share on June 11, 2026 and began trading on Nasdaq as **SPCX** on June 12, 2026, raising ~$75B (largest IPO ever) at ~$1.75–1.77T; first-day close $160.95 (+19%) put market cap at ~$2.1T. ## KQ3. Starship status in 2026: flights, failures, Artemis III/HLS timing, operational use

### Takeaway
Starship V3 debuted in 2026 with three flights (May 22, July 24, Sept 28): ships have succeeded with increasing completeness (payload deploys, in-space relight, intact splashdown, and reportedly first orbit on Flight 14), but Super Heavy was lost on both Flights 12 and 13, triggering an FAA mishap grounding May 27–~July 13. Flight 15 (first ship tower-catch attempt) is provisionally NET Oct 19, 2026. NASA restructured Artemis in Feb 2026: Artemis III is now a 2027 LEO docking test with Starship HLS and Blue Moon; first crewed landing slips to Artemis IV (early 2028). Propellant-transfer and uncrewed HLS landing demos remain unscheduled as of Sept 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Flight 12 (V3 debut), May 22, 2026, from Starbase Pad 2: Ship 39 flew planned suborbital trajectory, deployed 22 payloads (20 Starlink V3 mass simulators + 2 heat-shield inspection sats), lost one engine on ascent, cancelled relight, splashed down in Indian Ocean; booster lost engines, boostback failed, hard impact in Gulf — [Space.com live](https://www.space.com/news/live/spacex-starship-flight-12-launch-updates-may-22-2026); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/22/science/live-news/spacex-starship-flight-12-version-3-launch); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starship_flight_test_12)
- FAA declared Flight 12 a mishap on May 27, 2026, grounding V3 pending a SpaceX-led investigation — [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/faa-grounds-spacexs-starship-v3-megarocket-after-flight-12-mishap); [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/05/27/faa-requires-spacex-led-mishap-investigation-before-resumption-of-starship-launches/)
- Investigation closed ~July 13, 2026; probable causes: heat effects on propulsion components during ascent and erroneous engine-alarm settings; FAA cleared Flight 13 — [Starship fandom wiki](https://starship-spacex.fandom.com/wiki/Starship_Flight_Test_13) (unverified); [FAA statements page](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/statements/general-statements)

## KQ4. Direct-to-cell, spectrum (EchoStar 2025, 2026 deals) and what it means for ASTS, IRDM, Globalstar

### Takeaway
SpaceX now owns (pending final transfer by Nov 2027) ~65 MHz of licensed low/mid-band spectrum from EchoStar (~$17B, half in SpaceX stock), FCC-approved May 12, 2026 with waivers for terrestrial + satellite use, and is reportedly (FT, June 27, 2026) weighing a direct-to-consumer "Starlink Mobile" with its own small cells. Starlink D2C has ~650 satellites and T-Satellite is live ($10/mo add-on). The competitive response has been consolidation: Amazon is buying Globalstar (~$10.8–11.6B, April 14, 2026; Apple stake included), Rocket Lab is buying Iridium ($8B, June 29, 2026), and AST SpaceMobile is behind schedule (~45 sats targeted for early 2027 after an April 2026 orbit shortfall).
