## Key Question 1: Is US government space spending a tailwind or headwind in FY2027? Which listed companies benefit?

### Takeaway
Split picture: NASA (esp. science) is a headwind in the request but Congress is defending a flat ~$24.4B topline, while Space Force is a very large tailwind on paper (request ~$71B vs ~$32B FY26) — but a big share (and nearly all of Golden Dome) sits in a not-yet-introduced reconciliation bill, and the government is running on a CR through Dec 11, 2026. Clear beneficiaries are defense-space primes and new-space contractors with SDA/SBI/SDN awards (SpaceX, Lockheed, L3Harris, Northrop, Rocket Lab, Anduril, Sierra Space); science/exploration-exposed names face cuts.

### Cited Findings

**NASA FY2027 request vs Congress**
- The White House FY2027 request asks $18.8B for NASA, a 23% cut; one analysis puts the science reduction at ~47% (~$3.4B), and the request lists 40+ missions for termination but names only Mars Sample Return and SERVIR — [Space.com](https://www.space.com/astronomy/nasa-science-faces-very-serious-threat-from-new-white-house-budget-scientists-say); [Astrobites, Apr 16 2026](https://astrobites.org/2026/04/16/budget-request-fy2027/); [Eos](https://eos.org/research-and-developments/fy2027-budget-request-slashes-billions-in-science-funding)
- The request would cut NASA $5.609B (23%) below FY2026 enacted and eliminate STEM Engagement — [Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/articles/analyzing-the-fy-2027-nasa-budget-request)
- At an Apr 22, 2026 hearing, House Science Committee members of both parties said Congress will almost assuredly reject the 23% cut — [SpacePolicyOnline](https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/house-science-committee-opposes-cuts-in-nasas-fy2027-budget-request/)
- House Appropriations advanced its FY2027 CJS bill May 13, 2026 (32-28 party-line): NASA topline flat at $24.4B (excluding OBBBA supplemental), Exploration +$1.14B, Science Mission Directorate −17% ($7.25B → $6.0B) — [Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/articles/house-appropriators-advance-key-nasa-funding-bill); [AGU Bridge, May 26 2026](https://thebridge.agu.org/2026/05/26/fy2027-appropriations-update-nasa-noaa-and-nsf/); [House Appropriations](https://appropriations.house.gov/news/press-releases/committee-approves-fy27-commerce-justice-science-and-related-agencies)
- Congress rejected the similar FY2026 request and enacted ~$24.4B for NASA — [KeepTrack](https://keeptrack.space/deep-dive/isaacman-nasa-budget)
- As of early Sept 2026 none of the 12 FY2027 appropriations bills had cleared Congress and the Senate Appropriations Committee had not reported any — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/tracking-fy-2027-defense-appropriations-reconciliation-and-supplemental-request)
- CR: H.R. 6500 (Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027) passed Senate 90-6 (Aug 8), House 370-48 (Sept 1), signed Sept 2, 2026 (Fox reports Sept 3); funds government at FY2026 levels through Dec 11, 2026, deferring FY27 decisions past the midterms — [AACOM](https://www.aacom.org/news-reports/news/2026/09/08/president-trump-signs-continuing-resolution-to-fund-government-through-december-11); [NASFAA](https://www.nasfaa.org/news-item/39750/Trump_Signs_Short-Term_Spending_Bill_for_FY_2027); [SpacePolicyOnline](https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/house-clears-fy2027-cr-now-to-the-president/)

**Space Force FY2027**
- FY27 Space Force request is $71.1B — [DefenseScoop, Apr 28 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/28/space-force-space-data-network/); trade press cites $31.86B FY26 enacted as comparison — [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/04/golden-dome-spending-plan-fy27-budget-space-force/)

- Space Data Network (SDN): ~$1.5B R&D requested in FY27, all from reconciliation; Tranche 3 Transport Layer "is not funded" in FY27 and is folded into the SDN backbone — [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/28/space-force-space-data-network/); [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/04/space-force-shifts-from-sda-transport-layer-to-new-space-data-network-backbone-for-orbital-relay/)

- Artemis II launched Apr 1, 2026 and splashed down Apr 10, 2026 (first crewed lunar flyby in 50+ years); crew received the Congressional Space Medal of Honor Aug 28, 2026 — [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-ii/); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-artemis-ii-crew-space-medal/)

### Inferences
- Net FY2027 federal demand for the listed space sector is positive in dollar terms (Space Force +$39B request dwarfs the −$5.6B NASA request), but the quality of that tailwind is low: ~$12B of Space Force growth and ~$17B of Golden Dome depend on a reconciliation bill that had not been introduced as of Sept 2026, and the CR means no new-start money until at least Dec 11, 2026 (and likely a lame-duck omnibus or another CR).
- Listed beneficiaries by line item: SDA Tracking (LMT, LHX, RKLB, NOC), SBI/SDN/SB-AMTI (SPCX, LMT, NOC, RTX, BAH, GD; private: Anduril, True Anomaly), tracking sensor vehicles (LHX, Sierra Space-private). Listed names exposed to NASA science/exploration cuts: Redwire, Intuitive Machines (CLPS), Voyager (Starlab timing slipped to 2027 award).
- The CLD award slip from "early 2026" to "spring 2027" removes a 2026 catalyst for Voyager Technologies and station-supply-chain names.

## Key Question 2: Status of Golden Dome contracts as of Oct 2026 — who won what, dollar amounts?

### Takeaway
Awards so far are OTA prototype frameworks rather than production: a $3.2B-ceiling SBI prototype pool across 12 companies (Apr 24, 2026), SpaceX reported at ~$6.45B across SB-AMTI ($4.16B) and SDN backbone ($2.29B), and July 2026 tracking-sensor awards to L3Harris (≤$955M) and Sierra Space (≤$798M). Program cost is officially $185B (vs $175B initially) while CBO says $1.2T over 20 years; FY27 funding ($17.9B) is ~96% reconciliation-dependent.

### Cited Findings
- Space-Based Interceptor (SBI) prototypes: Space Force named 12 companies on Apr 24, 2026 — Anduril, Booz Allen Hamilton, General Dynamics Mission Systems, GITAI USA, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Quindar, Raytheon, Sci-Tec, SpaceX, True Anomaly, Turion Space — under OTA agreements worth up to a combined $3.2B; industry must demonstrate initial capability integrated into Golden Dome by 2028 — [DefenseScoop, Apr 24 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/24/golden-dome-space-based-interceptor-missile-defense-contractors/); [Via Satellite, Apr 26 2026](https://www.satellitetoday.com/government-military/2026/04/26/space-force-awards-3-2-billion-to-12-companies-for-space-based-interceptor-work/); [Inside Defense](https://insidedefense.com/insider/dod-awards-12-firms-32-billion-golden-dome-space-based-interceptors); [Fortune, Apr 25 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/04/25/spacex-anduril-lockheed-raytheon-northrop-golden-dome-interceptor-contracts/). One outlet counted 11 firms — [The Register, Apr 27 2026](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/27/us_names_firms_to_develop/). Per-company SBI dollar amounts were not broken out in any source found.
- Booz Allen's SBI prototype award is part of the same $3.2B framework, not a separate amount — [The Defense Post, May 6 2026](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/05/06/us-golden-dome-booz-allen/)
- SpaceX: reported $4.16B for Space-Based Airborne Moving Target Indicator (SB-AMTI) plus $2.29B for the Space Data Network (SDN) Backbone, $6.45B combined — [AeroMorning](https://aeromorning.com/en/spacex-wins-6-45b-to-build-golden-domes-space-layer/) (single-source figures; the SpaceX-centric SDN structure is corroborated qualitatively by [Quilty Space](https://www.quiltyspace.com/post/welcome-to-the-space-x-data-network) and [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/04/space-force-shifts-from-sda-transport-layer-to-new-space-data-network-backbone-for-orbital-relay/))
- July 2026 awards: L3Harris up to $955M and Sierra Space up to $798M for tracking sensor vehicles — [GovConWire Golden Dome tracker, July 2026](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/golden-dome-july-2026-missile-defense-ussf-space-based-interceptor)
- Cost: initial $175B estimate raised by $10B to $185B in March 2026, with the increment going to space capabilities (Gen. Guetlein) — [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/golden-dome-cost-estimate-rises-to-185-billion-as-pentagon-expands-space-layer/); [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5789969-golden-dome-missile-defense-cost/)
- CBO (May 2026): $1.2T over 20 years, assuming a 7,800-satellite SBI constellation plus expanded ground systems; Guetlein rejected the figure as based on outdated assumptions — [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/defense-news/2026/05/cbo-estimates-golden-dome-could-cost-1-2-trillion-over-20-years/); [Via Satellite, May 15 2026](https://www.satellitetoday.com/government-military/2026/05/15/what-will-the-golden-dome-cost-gen-guetlein-pushes-back-on-cbo-cost-estimate/); [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/golden-dome-chief-pushes-back-on-1-2-trillion-cbo-estimate/)
- FY27 funding: DOD requested $17.1B of the $17.9B Golden Dome total via reconciliation rather than discretionary appropriations; CRS cites $17.5B; a CR is problematic because Golden Dome has no prior-year discretionary rate to extend; the $350B reconciliation bill had not been introduced and "many are skeptical it could pass" — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/tracking-fy-2027-defense-appropriations-reconciliation-and-supplemental-request); [CRS IF13264, Jul 8 2026](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/PDF/IF13264/IF13264.1.pdf); [DoD FY2027 Budget Overview](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/Documents/defbudget/FY2027/FY2027_Budget_Request_Overview_Book.pdf); [Defense-Aerospace ("$17.5B requested, $400M written down")](https://www.defense-aerospace.com/golden-dome-fy2027-400m-written-down/)
- Space Force programs supporting Golden Dome saw a large FY27 boost — [Breaking Defense, Apr 2026](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/04/golden-dome-spending-plan-fy27-budget-space-force/)

### Inferences
- Contract flow to date (~$3.2B SBI ceiling + ~$6.45B SpaceX + ~$1.75B sensors + $3.5B SDA T3 tracking) is ~$15B of ceilings/awards against a $185B program — i.e., the equity market is pricing a pipeline, not booked revenue. Prototype OTAs with ceilings are not guaranteed obligations.
- SpaceX is the single largest Golden Dome space-layer beneficiary (SDN + SB-AMTI + SBI participation), which structurally diverts "Golden Dome upside" away from smaller listed names and toward SPCX.
- Reconciliation dependency means Golden Dome money is politically contingent on the post-midterm Congress; a failed reconciliation would make FY27 Golden Dome spending near-zero unless appropriators move it to the base bill.

### Gaps
- No Golden Dome award announcements dated Aug–Oct 2026 were found in search; the latest tracker found is July 2026. Whether further SBI down-selects or ground-layer awards occurred in Q3 2026 is unconfirmed.
- No per-company dollar split for the $3.2B SBI pool.
- No Sept/Oct 2026 update to the $185B estimate.

## Key Question 3: What did the Fed do Jul–Oct 2026, how did 10-yr yields move, and how did space stocks trade in each phase?

### Takeaway
The Fed held 9-3 on Jul 29 (three hawkish dissents), Warsh turned explicitly hawkish at Jackson Hole (Aug 28), and the FOMC hiked 25bp to 3.75–4.00% on Sept 16 (12-0, first hike since 2023); the 10-yr went from ~5% pre-meeting to a 5.27% peak on Sept 29 (highest since 2007) and sat at 5.08–5.19% in mid-Oct. Pre-profit space names sold off in every phase: AST −15.5% in Jackson Hole week, UFO −16.6% in Q3, Rocket Lab "imploded for 3 months," and the sector fell again in the second week of Oct on rising yields plus idiosyncratic hits.
